How Allocation Works

  1. Select the student's target semester and section.
  2. Enter one or multiple existing student usernames.
  3. Choose academic year and term.
  4. The system checks roles, capacity and existing allocations.
  5. Students are enrolled in the section's active course offerings.

Semester Promotion

To promote students, select a section belonging to the next semester and specify the new academic period.

Allocations from previous academic periods will remain in the allocation history. The Section Transfer checkbox is not required for promotion into a new academic period.
